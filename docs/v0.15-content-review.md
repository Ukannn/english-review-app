# v0.15 content acceptance

The three complete packages in `tests/fixtures/english-v3-lessons.json` use eight expressions selected from recorded v2 difficulty/hint evidence. Only generic expressions are retained: there are no personal answers, identities, messages or source contexts. Fixture identifiers are synthetic. Each package has eight independent tasks and two responses; reading is a separate material object.

| Package | Theme / form | Words | Editorial assessment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A quieter Saturday | Life / dialogue | 143 | A friend changes a walk to nearby coffee. Illness and energy expressions fit naturally. Both replies have a recipient and purpose. |
| Choosing a place for lunch | Life / passage | 138 | Outdoor availability motivates an indoor choice. The personal reply can choose any reasonable venue. |
| A smaller first step | Work / dialogue | 144 | A delivery compromise and bounded help request motivate the more formal phrases. Formal vocabulary is appropriate here, not forced into casual chat. |

Editorial review checked naturalness, coherent cause and response, public/private field separation, explicit answer forms, target association and grading consistency. The work package is harder: its first reply can use simple language and need not reproduce the formal phrases. Reference answers are examples, not unique required strings. For example, “I’d choose the café near the station” can communicate successfully without testing “go with”. A correct chunk is not penalized for lacking a subject or punctuation.

The sample reviews exercise the full default count; actual queues may contain fewer tasks, gap support or up to two independent responses based on recent evidence. All eight sample review items are whole-recall tasks because the sample fixes no claim about a learner's independent success history. The examples do not establish that eight tasks take exactly 15–20 minutes. Burden and delayed retrieval require the user's next ten completed lessons; content review and passing schema validation cannot establish instructional effectiveness.

Existing potentially leaked v2 items remain a limitation. This release hides their private rubric in the UI but does not rescore history or roll back due dates.
